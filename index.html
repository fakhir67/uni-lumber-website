About Uni-Lumber

Sabah-based since 1996.

Uni-Lumber Sdn. Bhd. is a Kota Kinabalu-based exporter and manufacturer of tropical sawn timber.

Its supply network connects two owned mills: Irama Ramai Sdn. Bhd. in Keningau and Gold Rank Sdn. Bhd. in Tawau. Public Sabah TLAS assessment records describe Sabah-sourced round logs being directed to these facilities for conversion, with Uni-Lumber handling local sales and exports.
