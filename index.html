Company

A connected timber business, grounded in Sabah.

Based in Kota Kinabalu, Uni-Lumber Sdn. Bhd. is assessed as an exporter and importer of sawn timber.

Public forestry assessments record that the company purchases round logs from Sabah and directs material to its two owned sawmills: Irama Ramai Sdn. Bhd. in Keningau and Gold Rank Sdn. Bhd. in Tawau. Uni-Lumber manages sales to local and documented international markets.
